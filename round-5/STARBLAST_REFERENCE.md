Starblast is the more useful reference for the experiment’s 3D presentation layer. Its official browser surface remains at [starblast.io](https://starblast.io/), and its product description identifies a fast top-down multiplayer space shooter built around asteroid mining, ship upgrades, distinct ship models, weapons, team stations, gems, and procedurally generated arenas. See the [Starblast Steam product page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/673260/Starblast/).

This is visual-language and interaction evidence, not a cloning target. `HOSTILE_SPACE_REFERENCE.md` owns persistent-world lineage and information roles; the approved V2 images remain the only visual oracle scored in Round 5A.

## Preserve as 3D presentation invariants

- Use low-poly faceted geometry whose lighting reveals volume at ordinary gameplay distance.
- Keep a tactical top-down or slightly oblique camera while allowing visible height, overlap, and landmark scale.
- Make the player’s heading obvious through nose shape, engine placement, exhaust, and weapon direction.
- Use distinct silhouettes and restrained faction color so ships remain identifiable without labels.
- Let stars, nebulae, debris, asteroids, and landmarks create depth and motion reference without competing with contacts.
- Use short world-anchored trails, bolts, and emissions to explain motion and combat state.
- Keep radar, hull/energy/resources, target state, and contact labels legible at the edges of the world view.
- Allow a station or planet-scale structure to establish world scale in appropriate scenes while preserving the opening-frame hierarchy fixed by the V2 oracle.

## Adapt rather than copy

- Use a fixed-yaw slightly oblique presentation for this benchmark instead of reproducing Starblast’s exact camera or controls.
- Keep the Round 5 compact HUD rather than its large leaderboard, faction panel, segmented meters, or desktop-sized radar.
- Translate ship and station complexity into original assemblies of boxes, cones, cylinders, toruses, rings, and shards.
- Use our cyan/coral/steel/amber visual contract rather than Starblast faction themes, icons, wireframes, type, or panel shapes.
- Preserve negative space and mobile portrait usability; do not treat dense 16:9 desktop layouts as responsive guidance.
- Keep threat-gated automatic combat and magnetic salvage from the functional contract rather than copying Starblast’s manual fire/mining loop.

## Exclude from implementation

- Exact ship trees, ship silhouettes, stations, asteroids, maps, faction colors, icons, meters, radar, labels, names, fonts, or trade dress.
- Screenshot pixels as textures, tracing sources, generated-image inputs, or runtime assets.
- Literal UI geometry, leaderboard content, room links, game-mode language, or control labels.
- A station dominating the frozen opening frame merely because it dominates one supplied encounter screenshot.
